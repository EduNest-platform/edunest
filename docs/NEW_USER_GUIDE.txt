STEP 1: DOWNLOAD & INSTALL EDUNEST APK
=======================================

System Requirements:
- Android Version: 6.0 or higher
- Storage Space: At least 50MB free space
- Internet: Required for setup and syncing

Installation Steps:

1. Download the APK:
   **Option A - From WhatsApp/Direct Share:**
   - Receive the EduNest.apk file via WhatsApp or direct share
   - Tap to download and save to your device
   
   **Option B - From Download Page:**
   - Visit: edunest.me/EduNest-download
   - Tap "Download APK" button for Android
   - Save the EduNest.apk file to your device

2. Open with Package Installer:
   - Once download is complete, tap the downloaded file
   - Select "Package installer" when prompted with "Open with" dialog
   
   📱 Screens you will see:
   • "Open with Package Installer" popup
   • EduNest.apk installation progress screen
   
   ⏳ Important: Wait 4–5 minutes for installation to complete. 
   Do not close the screen during installation.

3. Grant Permissions:
   - Follow the on-screen installation prompts
   - Allow necessary permissions when asked
   - Tap "Install" to complete the process

STEP 2: CREATE YOUR ACCOUNT
============================

Simple Registration:

1. Launch EduNest:
   - Tap the EduNest icon
   - Wait for the app to load

2. Sign Up:
   - Tap "Sign Up" or "Create New Account"
   - Fill in these details:
     • Your Full Name
     • Email Address
     • Password (minimum 6 characters)
     • Confirm Password

3. Email Verification:
   - Check your email inbox (and spam folder)
   - Click the verification link
   - Return to app and login with your credentials

STEP 3: COMPLETE YOUR ACADEMIC PROFILE
=======================================

After login, you'll set up your academic information:

1. College Selection:
   - Choose your college from the dropdown
   - Search if you don't see it listed

2. Branch & Semester:
   - Select your branch/department
   - Choose current semester

3. Roll Number:
   - Enter your roll number (important for attendance)
   - This helps professors track your attendance

4. Save Profile:
   - Tap "Continue" to save your profile
   - You'll be taken to the home screen

DESKTOP APP INSTALLATION (WINDOWS USERS)
=========================================

For Windows/Desktop Users:

Download Process:
1. Visit: https://edunest.me/EduNest-download-page/
2. Click "Download EduNest for Windows"
3. Save the installer file to your computer

Installation Steps:
1. **Run the Installer**:
   - Double-click the downloaded EduNest installer
   - You may see a Windows Defender warning

2. **Handle Windows Defender Warning**:
   📱 You will see: "Windows protected your PC" message
   - This is normal for new applications
   - Click "More info" link
   - Then click "Run anyway" button
   - This allows the EduNest installer to proceed

3. **Complete Installation**:
   - Follow the installation wizard
   - Choose installation location (or use default)
   - Wait for installation to complete
   - Launch EduNest from desktop shortcut

Note: The Windows Defender warning appears because EduNest is a new application. It's completely safe to proceed with "Run anyway".

QUICK TROUBLESHOOTING
======================

Common Issues:
- **App won't load**: Check your internet connection
- **Login problems**: Verify email and password, check if email is verified
- **Attendance not syncing**: Ensure you entered correct roll number
- **Files won't download**: Check storage space and permissions
- **Forgot password**: Use "Forgot Password" link on login screen
