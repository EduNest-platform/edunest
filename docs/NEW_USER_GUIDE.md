 # 🎓 EduNest - New User Quick Start Guide
## Welcome to Your Smart Learning Journey!

### 📱 **Step 1: Download & Install EduNest APK**

#### **Download Requirements:**
- **Android Version:** 6.0 (API level 23) or higher
- **Storage Space:** At least 100MB free space
- **Internet Connection:** Required for initial setup and data sync

#### **Installation Steps:**
1. **Enable Unknown Sources** (if not already enabled):
   - Go to `Settings > Security > Unknown Sources`
   - Toggle ON "Allow installation from unknown sources"
   - Or for newer Android: `Settings > Apps & Notifications > Special App Access > Install Unknown Apps`

2. **Download the APK:**
   - Download the latest EduNest APK from the provided link
   - Ensure download completes successfully

3. **Install the App:**
   - Locate the downloaded APK file in your Downloads folder
   - Tap on the APK file to begin installation
   - Follow the on-screen prompts
   - Grant necessary permissions when prompted

---

### 🔐 **Step 2: Create Your Account**

#### **Sign Up Process:**
1. **Open EduNest App**
   - Tap the EduNest icon on your home screen
   - Wait for the app to load (first time may take a few seconds)

2. **Choose Sign Up**
   - On the welcome screen, tap "Sign Up"
   - You'll see the registration form

3. **Enter Your Details:**
   ```
   📧 Email Address: Use your college/personal email
   🔑 Password: Create a strong password (min 8 characters)
   👤 Full Name: Enter your complete name
   📱 Phone Number: Your mobile number (optional)
   ```

4. **Verify Your Email:**
   - Check your email inbox for verification link
   - Click the verification link
   - Return to the app and proceed

---

### 🎯 **Step 3: Complete Academic Profile Setup**

#### **Essential Information Required:**
1. **College Information:**
   - Select your college from the dropdown
   - If not listed, contact support to add your college

2. **Branch/Department:**
   - Choose your branch (Engineering, Arts, Commerce, etc.)
   - Select your specific department

3. **Academic Details:**
   - Current Semester/Year
   - Roll Number (important for attendance tracking)
   - Student ID (if different from roll number)

4. **Profile Completion:**
   - Upload profile picture (optional but recommended)
   - Add any additional academic information

### 🔧 **Step 6: Troubleshooting Common Issues**

#### **Login Issues:**
- **Forgot Password:** Use "Forgot Password" link on login screen
- **Email Not Verified:** Check spam folder for verification email
- **Account Locked:** Contact support if login attempts fail

#### **Performance Issues:**
- **Slow Loading:** Ensure stable internet connection
- **App Crashes:** Restart app or reinstall if persistent
- **Storage Full:** Clear app cache or free up device storage

#### **Sync Issues:**
- **Data Not Loading:** Check internet connection
- **Attendance Not Syncing:** Verify with professor's records
- **Marks Not Showing:** Contact academic office
